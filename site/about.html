WHAT THIS IS AND WHO MAKES IT

About Botbuilder

Botbuilder is a set of small printed robots for the controllers, motors and cameras you already own, with a recipe for each: the files to print, the parts, the wiring and the software.

What you can do here

  • Build yours: pick a finished build, or tell it what parts you have, and get the recipe as a page, a PDF or a zip with the files.
  • Biped, Two-wheeled and Walkers: each robot type in 3D, with the numbers behind it.
  • Flash: the commands or the program for your controller.
  • Control: drive from a game controller, in the page first.
  • Take the tour for a two-minute walk through all of it.

Every recipe says how far it has got: simulated, checked in the digital assembly, or designed. Admin lists the builds and parts the maker has marked after printing them. The detail is in the status document linked in the footer.

Who makes it

Botbuilder is made by Eoin Jordan. Two related projects of his:

  • Sketchbot: a drawing robot arm on the Arduino UNO Q and the Braccio kit. The Arms tab opens it.
  • Mindstorms Robot Creator: writes and loads programs for LEGO hubs. The recipes for LEGO builds open their programs in it.

Report a problem or contribute

Found a mistake, a part that does not fit, or a build you would like to see? Open an issue on GitHub. Say which build it is; the Copy link button on a recipe gives its address.

The source holds everything the site shows: the printable files, the data behind the recipes and the scripts that make the pictures. Changes are welcome as pull requests.
